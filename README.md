# MythBusters
IMAD5112 Assignment 2

## Purpose
The app helps users test their knowledge of everyday life hacks by separating real tips from common myths. It’s designed to be fun, interactive, and educational, encouraging learning through quick true/false questions, instant feedback, and a final review of answers.

---

## User Experience
- **Simple Flow**: Start screen leads into a series of true/false questions.  
- **Instant Feedback**: Pop‑ups provide immediate responses after each answer.  
- **Score Tracking**: A live score counter motivates users as they progress.  
- **Personalized Messages**: Score screen gives encouragement based on performance.  
- **Review Feature**: Users can scroll through all their answers with correct solutions.  

---

## Conclusion
The app balances simplicity and engagement. By combining quick questions, supportive feedback, and a review system, it creates a lightweight but effective tool that makes knowledge checks enjoyable and motivating.
